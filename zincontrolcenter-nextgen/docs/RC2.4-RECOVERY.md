# RC2.4 Recovery

RC2.4 restores the accepted application shell and dashboard renderers after the RC2.3 regression. It retains clock-in/out, role-based landing redirects, immersive mode, analog clock layouts, themes, command search, and widget layout persistence.

## Staging checks
- Network Command shows four KPIs and four draggable widgets.
- Staff Hub shows clock, staff KPIs, online staff, working staff, leave, and directory.
- Profile shows avatar, employment data, timesheet total, notifications, shift, and leave.
- Support shows ticket KPIs and the role-visible queue or a truthful empty state.
- HR shows HR KPIs and staff records or a truthful empty state.
- Non-Super-Admin staff land on the correct role workspace.
